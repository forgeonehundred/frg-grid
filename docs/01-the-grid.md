## Why a grid?

A business is easy to oversimplify — into a single score, or a flat checklist.
Both lose the thing that matters most: **structure**. A company that's brilliant
at winning customers but bleeding cash is very different from one that's
profitable but irrelevant — yet a single number can rate them the same.

The Forge 25-Grid keeps the structure. It says every part of business health
lives at the intersection of **which part of the business** you mean (a pillar)
and **what kind of health** you mean (a dimension).

![The Forge 25-Grid](../assets/grid.svg)

## The two axes

**Columns — the 5 pillars.** The major, mutually-reinforcing parts of any
organization: **C**ommerce, **O**perations, **P**eople, **F**inance,
**I**ntegrity. ([Defined here →](02-the-five-pillars.md))

**Rows — the 5 dimensions.** The same five views, applied to every pillar:

| # | Dimension | The question it asks |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Availability | What do we have ready *right now*? |
| 2 | Adaptability | How easily can we change? |
| 3 | Effectiveness | Are we getting the *right* results? |
| 4 | Efficiency | How much output per unit of input? |
| 5 | Sustainability | Can we keep this up over time? |

Cross the 5 pillars with the 5 dimensions and you get **25 domains** — each one a
specific, nameable facet of business health.

## Reading a domain code

![Reading a domain code](../assets/cell-anatomy.svg)

A code is **`<PillarLetter><DimensionNumber>`**:

- the **letter** picks the column (pillar): `C O P F I`
- the **number** picks the row (dimension): `1`–`5`

So:

- `F4` → **F**inance × `4` Efficiency → **Profitability**
- `C1` → **C**ommerce × `1` Availability → **Demand**
- `P3` → **P**eople × `3` Effectiveness → **Engagement**
- `I5` → **I**ntegrity × `5` Sustainability → **Trust**

## The full board

|  | **C** Commerce | **O** Operations | **P** People | **F** Finance | **I** Integrity |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1 · Availability** | C1 Demand | O1 Capacity | P1 Workforce | F1 Cash | I1 Transparency |
| **2 · Adaptability** | C2 Competitiveness | O2 Responsiveness | P2 Mobility | F2 Liquidity | I2 Alignment |
| **3 · Effectiveness** | C3 Desirability | O3 Performance | P3 Engagement | F3 ROI | I3 Accountability |
| **4 · Efficiency** | C4 Conversion | O4 Optimization | P4 Utilization | F4 Profitability | I4 Rigor |
| **5 · Sustainability** | C5 Loyalty | O5 Continuity | P5 Belonging | F5 Solvency | I5 Trust |

Read a **column** top-to-bottom for the full picture of one pillar. Read a **row**
left-to-right to see one kind of health across the whole business.
