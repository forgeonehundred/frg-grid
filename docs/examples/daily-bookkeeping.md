# 🍽️ Daily Bookkeeping

[← Examples index](README.md) · [Back to README](../../README.md)

*Scenario: **Restaurant Franchise** · decision 1*

---

## The decision

> The franchise owner decides to **record every transaction in the books at the
> close of each day**, instead of batching paperwork up weekly.

A small operational habit — but watch how far it travels across the grid.

![Daily bookkeeping mapped onto the grid](../../assets/example-bookkeeping.svg)

## Cell by cell

| Cell | | Role | w | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **F4 Profitability** | 🟩 + | primary | 1.0 | Catching expense leaks the same day they happen protects the profit kept on every dollar of sales. |
| **O4 Optimization** | 🟩 + | primary | 1.0 | Fresh daily numbers let the operation tune ordering and labor against real facts instead of stale guesses. |
| **F3 ROI** | 🟩 + | secondary | 0.8 | Knock-on: tighter cost visibility raises the return earned on money already deployed. |
| **P1 Workforce** | 🟥 − | tradeoff | 1.0 | A manager closing the books every night is hours pulled off the floor and off the team. |
| **O2 Responsiveness** | 🟥 − | tradeoff | 0.8 | Knock-on: a nightly paperwork ritual slows the close and the start of the next service. |

## What it shows about the grid

- **One decision, three domains.** A bookkeeping habit lands on **Finance**
  (F4, F3), **Operations** (O4, O2), and **People** (P1) at once.
- **The spine.** The two primaries are **F4 Profitability** and **O4
  Optimization** — the *Efficiency* row (4) of Finance and Operations. Daily
  numbers are fundamentally an **efficiency** move: more insight per unit of
  effort. (In the game, O4 is treated as the board's gravitational center.)
- **The price is paid in People and tempo.** The strengths in Finance/Operations
  are bought with **P1 Workforce** time and **O2 Responsiveness**. Whether the
  trade is worth it depends on the situation — if the team is already stretched,
  this "free" insight isn't free.

> **The takeaway:** the grid turns "should we do our books daily?" from a yes/no
> into a visible exchange — *buy Profitability + Optimization with Workforce
> hours.* Now the owner can decide with eyes open.
