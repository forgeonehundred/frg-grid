# The Five Pillars

[← The Grid](01-the-grid.md) · [Back to README](../README.md) · Next: [The 25 Domains →](03-the-25-domains.md)

---

The five **pillars** are the columns of the grid — the major parts of any
organization. They lock down the vocabulary: when someone says "this is a People
problem" or "that's a Commerce win," everyone knows exactly which part of the
business they mean.

They're deliberately broad and mutually reinforcing: Commerce creates demand,
Operations fulfils it, People do the work, Finance keeps score and funds it, and
Integrity holds the whole thing together honestly.

Each pillar splits into its five **domains** (one per [dimension](01-the-grid.md)).
The domain names are in brackets — full definitions are in
[The 25 Domains](03-the-25-domains.md).

---

## C · Commerce 🟦

> Market-facing — driving customer acquisition, retention, and value creation
> through positioning, pricing, and customer experience.

Everything about *winning and keeping customers*: knowing the market, standing
out in it, being wanted, converting attention into revenue, and earning loyalty.

| Domain | Name |
|---|---|
| C1 | **Demand** — exposure to market needs & purchasing intent |
| C2 | **Competitiveness** — reconfiguring position to stay relevant |
| C3 | **Desirability** — how well the offer matches what customers want |
| C4 | **Conversion** — turning attention into paying customers |
| C5 | **Loyalty** — long-term, repeat engagement and preference |

---

## O · Operations 🟨

> Internal execution — transforming inputs into outputs through efficient
> processes, resource use, and service delivery.

The *engine room*: the capacity you can call on, how fast you adapt it, how lean
it runs, how reliably it performs, and whether it keeps running through trouble.

| Domain | Name |
|---|---|
| O1 | **Capacity** — usable operational resources ready to activate |
| O2 | **Responsiveness** — speed/flexibility reacting to new constraints |
| O3 | **Performance** — reliability in hitting output targets |
| O4 | **Optimization** — minimizing input per unit of output |
| O5 | **Continuity** — sustaining function despite disruption |

---

## P · People 🟩

> Human capital — talent acquisition, development, retention, and the culture
> that enables every other function.

The *humans* who make everything else possible: having the right people, moving
them where they're needed, using their time well, keeping them committed, and
giving them a place they belong.

| Domain | Name |
|---|---|
| P1 | **Workforce** — access to the people needed to operate & grow |
| P2 | **Mobility** — shifting, reskilling, redeploying talent |
| P3 | **Engagement** — emotional & cognitive commitment to the work |
| P4 | **Utilization** — applying time & skills to productive work |
| P5 | **Belonging** — durable sense of fit, purpose, safety |

---

## F · Finance 🟧

> Financial management — sustainable resource allocation, risk management, and
> value creation through financial decisions.

The *scorekeeper and fuel tank*: liquidity on hand, the flexibility to move
money, the return it earns, the profit kept, and long-term solvency.

| Domain | Name |
|---|---|
| F1 | **Cash** — immediate liquidity & room to maneuver |
| F2 | **Liquidity** — shifting financial resources/structure |
| F3 | **ROI** — return generated from a financial input |
| F4 | **Profitability** — ratio of earned income to costs |
| F5 | **Solvency** — meeting obligations & staying viable long-term |

---

## I · Integrity 🟪

> Ethics and governance — consistent values-based decisions, transparency, and
> durable stakeholder trust.

The *conscience and glue*: being transparent about decisions, staying aligned to
values under pressure, holding people accountable, keeping rigor without
bureaucracy, and earning trust.

| Domain | Name |
|---|---|
| I1 | **Transparency** — visibility of decisions & their rationale |
| I2 | **Alignment** — coherence of values and evolving actions |
| I3 | **Accountability** — identifying responsible actors, answerability |
| I4 | **Rigor** — principled behavior baked into processes |
| I5 | **Trust** — durable stakeholder confidence over time |
